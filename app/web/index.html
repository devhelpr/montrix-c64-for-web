About this version

A browser-native recreation of Montrix, using artwork and room data captured from the original C64 game. Code, design, concept, music and sound effects: Maikel vd Lisdonk. Graphics and design: Ramon vd Laar.

All 60 original rooms are included. Enemy behavior and synthesized sound are approximations. The original simulator build is preserved separately for reference. Private use for the game's owner.
